VIDEO 1: Weight of a Neutron Star
Script: A single teaspoon of this object weighs more than Mount Everest. A single teaspoon of a neutron star weighs about six billion tons. When a massive star explodes in a supernova, its core collapses under gravity, packing the mass of an entire sun into a sphere barely the size of a city. The atoms are crushed so tightly together that protons and electrons combine into pure neutrons. If you dropped just one sugar-cube-sized piece on Earth, it would fall straight through the crust toward the core.

VIDEO 5: Undersea Internet Cables
Script: 99% of global internet traffic does not travel through satellites. Almost all international internet data travels through physical fiber-optic cables resting directly on the ocean floor. Hundreds of these deep-sea lines span more than eight hundred thousand miles across the globe. Many are barely as thick as a garden hose, wrapped in protective steel wire, carrying every international video call, financial transfer, and stream at the speed of light.

VIDEO 7: Göbekli Tepe Mystery
Script: In 1994, an archaeologist uncovered a site that broke our history timeline. Göbekli Tepe is a massive stone temple complex in Turkey that dates back over eleven thousand years. That makes it thousands of years older than Stonehenge and the Pyramids. It was built by hunter-gatherers long before the invention of agriculture, pottery, or metal tools. Its discovery proved that prehistoric humans possessed complex social organization and monumental engineering skills far earlier than experts believed.

VIDEO 8: The Jungle Survivor
Script: In 1971, a teenager fell two miles from the sky into the Amazon and survived. Seventeen-year-old Juliane Koepcke was strapped into her airplane seat when lightning struck her commercial flight over Peru. She fell two miles into the dense canopy, surviving with a concussion and broken collarbone. Remembering survival advice from her father, she located a small stream and followed the water downstream for eleven days straight through the wilderness until local loggers found her alive.

VIDEO 9: The McGurk Illusion
Script: Your eyes can trick your ears into hearing words that were never spoken. This phenomenon is known as the McGurk effect. When you watch a video of a person mouthing 'ga-ga' while the actual recorded audio plays 'ba-ba', your brain merges the conflicting signals and makes you hear 'da-da'. The moment you close your eyes, the audio magically returns to 'ba-ba'. It proves that what you hear is actively constructed by what you see.
Caption: Can you really trust what your senses tell you? 🧠
